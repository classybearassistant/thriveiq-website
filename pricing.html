Questions

Is my health data private?

Yes. Your data is encrypted in transit and at rest. ThriveIQ will never sell or share your health data. You can delete all your data from the app at any time.

Do I need any hardware or devices?

No hardware required. ThriveIQ works entirely from your phone. You can optionally connect Apple Health on any plan. More wearables are on the roadmap.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. Cancel anytime from the app or App Store settings. Your data stays accessible until the end of your billing period and can always be exported.

Is ThriveIQ a medical device?

No. ThriveIQ is a personal wellness tracking tool, not a medical device. All content is educational. Always discuss your health data and symptoms with a qualified healthcare provider.

What's the difference between standard and functional ranges?

Standard lab ranges are set by testing large populations — they show what's common. Some functional medicine practitioners use narrower ranges. ThriveIQ shows both so you can have an informed conversation with your doctor about where you stand.
