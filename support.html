Support

How can we help?

Most answers are right here. Anything else — we're one email away.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple, so canceling takes about 30 seconds: open Settings on your iPhone → tap your name → Subscriptions → ThriveIQ → Cancel Subscription.

You'll keep Pro access until the end of your current billing period. Your data stays yours either way.

How does the 7-day free trial work?

Your trial gives you full Pro access for 7 days. If you don't cancel before the trial ends, your subscription begins automatically. You can cancel anytime during the trial in Settings → your name → Subscriptions, and you'll keep Pro until the trial's last day.

I was charged and want a refund.

All purchases and refunds are handled by Apple, not ThriveIQ. Request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com — sign in with your Apple ID, find the charge, and choose "Request a refund." Apple typically responds within 48 hours.

Is my health data private?

Yes. Your health data lives on your device. It's encrypted, it is never sold or shared, and there are no ads — ever. You can delete all of your data at any time from inside the app.

How do I delete my data or my account?

In the app, go to My Health → Settings and choose delete. This permanently removes your data. If you have any trouble, email us and we'll make sure it's done.

Where is my Health Conversation Guide?

Tap Build My Conversation Guide from the Insights tab. It gathers your logged meals, symptoms, labs, and patterns into a one-page summary — written in your voice — that you can save as a PDF and bring to your next appointment. Free includes one guide per month; Pro is unlimited.

Why is my meal scan limited?

The free plan includes 3 photo scans per day (manual logging is always unlimited). Pro removes the limit entirely. Your scan count resets each day at midnight.

The app crashed or something looks broken.

Sorry about that — we want to fix it fast. Email us with three things: what you were doing, your iPhone model, and your iOS version. Screenshots help enormously. Crashes are usually patched within days.

Can ThriveIQ tell me what's wrong with me?

No — and that's by design. ThriveIQ observes patterns in the data you log: what you ate, how you felt, what your numbers were. It never diagnoses, prescribes, or replaces your doctor. What it does is help you walk into your next appointment with organized evidence instead of a vague feeling — so you and your doctor can have a better conversation.

Can you help me interpret my lab results?

We can't interpret labs or give medical advice — for your safety and ours, that conversation belongs with your doctor, who knows your full picture. ThriveIQ shows you both standard and functional reference ranges, tracks your values over time, and adds them to your Health Conversation Guide so the conversation with your doctor starts in the right place.

When is Android coming?

It's on the roadmap, along with Oura, Garmin, Whoop, and Fitbit support. Join the waitlist and you'll be the first to know.
