Privacy Policy

Effective Date: July 1, 2026 · Last Updated: June 4, 2026

ThriveIQ is built on a foundation of trust. Your health data is yours. We do not sell it, share it with advertisers, or use it for any purpose other than making ThriveIQ work better for you.

This Privacy Policy explains how ThriveIQ ("we," "our," or "us") collects, uses, stores, and protects your information when you use the ThriveIQ mobile application and website at thriveiq.app ("the Service"). By using ThriveIQ you agree to the practices described in this policy.

1. Information We Collect

Information You Provide Directly

  • Account information: Name, email address, and password when you create an account
  • Profile information: Age, biological sex, height, weight, activity level, and dietary preferences you enter during onboarding
  • Health data: Meal photos, food quality scores, macro logs, symptom logs, bowel health logs, hydration logs, and biomarker values you enter manually
  • Medication and supplement information: Medications and supplements you log for food interaction checking
  • Lab results: Biomarker values you enter from your personal bloodwork
  • Communications: Messages you send to our support team

Information Collected Automatically

  • Usage data: Features you use, screens you visit, and actions you take within the app
  • Device information: Device type, operating system version, and app version
  • Crash reports: Anonymous error logs to help us fix bugs

Health Data — Special Notice

ThriveIQ collects sensitive health information including meal data, symptoms, biomarkers, and medication information. This data is protected with the highest level of care. We treat all health data as strictly confidential and never use it for advertising, marketing to third parties, or any purpose beyond providing and improving the ThriveIQ service to you.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Provide, operate, and improve the ThriveIQ app and its features
  • Generate personalized food quality scores, meal plans, and health insights
  • Calculate derived metrics including HOMA-IR, cholesterol ratios, and methylation patterns
  • Check meals against your logged medications for potential food interactions
  • Generate your Doctor Visit Report
  • Personalize your daily hydration goal based on your profile and logged symptoms
  • Send you important notifications about your account or app updates
  • Respond to your support requests
  • Analyze aggregate, anonymized usage patterns to improve the app
  • Comply with legal obligations

We do NOT use your health data to train AI models, sell to third parties, share with insurers or employers, or target you with advertising.

3. How We Store and Protect Your Data

Local Storage

By default, your meal logs, symptom data, biomarker values, and medication information are stored locally on your device. This data does not leave your device unless you enable cloud sync.

Cloud Storage (when sync is enabled)

When you enable cloud sync, your data is encrypted in transit using TLS 1.3 and stored encrypted at rest using AES-256 encryption through our database provider (Supabase). Your data is stored on servers located in the United States.

API Processing

When you use the photo meal analysis feature, your meal photo is sent to our secure backend server which processes it using the Anthropic Claude API to generate nutritional analysis. Meal photos are processed in real time and are not permanently stored on our servers after analysis is complete.

Security Measures

  • All data transmission is encrypted using TLS
  • Passwords are hashed and never stored in plain text
  • API keys are stored server-side and never exposed in the app
  • We conduct regular security reviews
  • Access to user data is strictly limited to essential personnel

4. Data Sharing and Third Parties

We do not sell your personal information. We share your data only in the following limited circumstances:

Service Providers

We use trusted third-party services to operate ThriveIQ. These providers are contractually bound to protect your data and may only use it to provide services to us:

  • Supabase — secure database and authentication
  • Anthropic — AI processing for meal photo analysis (photos processed in real time, not stored)
  • Apple — app distribution, in-app purchases, and HealthKit integration (governed by Apple's Privacy Policy)

Legal Requirements

We may disclose your information if required by law, court order, or government authority, or if we believe disclosure is necessary to protect the rights, property, or safety of ThriveIQ, our users, or the public.

Business Transfers

If ThriveIQ is acquired or merges with another company, your information may be transferred as part of that transaction. We will notify you before your information is transferred and becomes subject to a different privacy policy.

5. Apple HealthKit

If you choose to connect ThriveIQ with Apple HealthKit, we may read data such as steps, active calories, heart rate variability, and sleep data to personalize your hydration goals and insights. This data is used only within ThriveIQ and is never shared with third parties or used for advertising purposes, in compliance with Apple's HealthKit guidelines.

HealthKit data will not be used for advertising, sold to data brokers, or shared with insurance companies, employers, or any entity that might use it to make decisions about you.

6. Medical Disclaimer

ThriveIQ is for informational and personal wellness tracking purposes only. It is not a medical device and does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. Food quality scores, biomarker insights, symptom correlations, food-medication interaction flags, and all other content are educational and informational tools only. Always consult a qualified healthcare provider before making decisions about your health, diet, or medications.

7. Your Rights and Choices

Access and Portability

You can access and export all of your ThriveIQ data at any time from the Me tab in the app. Your Doctor Visit Report is one form of data export available to you.

Correction

You can edit or correct any information you've entered in ThriveIQ directly within the app at any time.

Deletion

You can delete individual entries (meals, symptoms, biomarker readings) at any time within the app. To delete your entire account and all associated data, go to Me → Settings → Delete Account, or contact us at privacy@thriveiq.app. We will delete your account and data within 30 days of your request.

Notifications

You can control all push notifications from ThriveIQ in your device's notification settings or within the app under Me → Settings → Notifications.

HealthKit

You can disconnect HealthKit access at any time through your iPhone's Health app under Sources.

8. Children's Privacy

ThriveIQ is not intended for use by children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, please contact us at privacy@thriveiq.app and we will delete it promptly.

9. California Privacy Rights (CCPA)

If you are a California resident, you have the right to know what personal information we collect, request deletion of your personal information, and opt out of the sale of your personal information. We do not sell personal information. To exercise your rights, contact us at privacy@thriveiq.app.

10. International Users

ThriveIQ is operated from the United States. If you access ThriveIQ from outside the United States, your information may be transferred to and processed in the United States, where privacy laws may differ from those in your country. By using ThriveIQ you consent to this transfer.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we make material changes, we will notify you through the app and update the "Last Updated" date at the top of this policy. Your continued use of ThriveIQ after changes take effect constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.
